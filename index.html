The Project

Who We Are?

We are a group of architecture students driven by passion, creativity, and a vision that goes beyond borders. Our goal is to grow, create, and share work that reaches a global audience and leaves a lasting impact.

"Architecture is more than buildings it is the balance between creativity, function, culture, and human experience."

We chose Islamic architecture because of its timeless beauty, rich identity, and unique spiritual and cultural value. Inspired by its remarkable details and harmony, we recreated Bayt al-Suhaymi as a 1:50 scale model to reflect the elegance and depth of this architectural style. Through this project, our goal was not only to build a model, but to express the brilliance, authenticity, and lasting impact of Islamic architecture in a way that connects the past with the present.

More Magic

Hidden within the heart of historic Cairo, Bayt al-Suhaymi stands as a timeless masterpiece where light dances through mashrabiyas, shadows carve stories across the walls, and every detail whispers the spirit of Islamic architecture. More than a historic house, it is a living reflection of elegance, intimacy, and cultural richness a place where architecture transforms into poetry. Inspired by its magic and authenticity, we recreated the house as a 1:50 scale model to capture not only its form, but also the soul and brilliance that make it unforgettable.
